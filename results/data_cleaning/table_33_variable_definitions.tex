severe\_reporting\_failure\_event & Reporting failure & Article 362(6) restatement or restatement without a modified audit opinion. & Component of primary failure \\
annual\_report\_missing & Data availability & One when the Annual Report record is not observed for a firm-year on the merged grid. & Missingness diagnostic; not treated as failure \\
